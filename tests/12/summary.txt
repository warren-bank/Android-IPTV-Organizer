purpose:
========
test that when EPG has multiple channels with the same ID,
and one (or more) passes a whitelist,
that it isn't removed when a subsequent channel does not pass whitelist filters.

desired behavior:
=================
keep the first such channel that passes the filters,
and ignore all subsequent channels having duplicate ID..
regardless of whether or not they would pass the filters.

=======
epg.xml
=======

| ID       | name        | passes | display in EPG |
|----------|-------------|--------|----------------|
| msnbc.us | MSNBC       | yes    | yes            |
| msnbc.us | !! MSNBC !! | yes    | no             |
| msnbc.us | MS Now      | no     | no             |
